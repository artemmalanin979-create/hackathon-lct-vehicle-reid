Description of the DATASET:
    1.       Urban Intersection and Highway Data: Approximately 12 hours of traffic videos synchronously captured from
             multiple vantage points at various urban intersections and along highways. Videos are 960p or better and
             most have been captured at 10 frames per second.
    2.       Metadata of Urban Intersection and Highway Data: Meta data includes GPS locations of cameras, natural
             language descriptions for vehicle tracks, camera calibration information and other derived data from videos.
    3.       Retail Dataset: Approximately 100,000 synthetic images of close to 100 objects available in retail stores, and
             approximately 10 minutes of videos capturing these objects.
